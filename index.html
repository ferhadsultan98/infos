About Aiprompt

This gallery collects carefully crafted prompts for AI image and video models so creators can work **faster** and more consistently.

Vision

The goal is to become a high‑quality prompt hub where designers, filmmakers and marketers can share, remix and discover the best ideas.

How it works

You can browse community uploads, save your favourite prompts and upload your own media with full prompt descriptions.

Privacy Policy

Aiprompt stores only the minimum **data** required to run the application and personalise your experience.

What is stored

Authentication is handled via Google; the app saves your UID, email, display name, avatar, cover image and your uploaded prompts.

How data is used

Data is used to display your profile, your uploads and bookmarks. It is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising.

Terms of Use

By using this **platform**, you agree to upload only content that you have rights to share and that complies with local laws.

Content ownership

You remain the owner of your media and prompts but grant permission to display them publicly inside the app.

Prohibited content

Illegal, hateful, explicit or otherwise harmful content may be removed, and accounts may be limited or banned.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

You can browse publicly without logging in, but bookmarks, uploads and AI Generate are available only after you sign in with Google.

Are prompts free to use?

Yes, prompts in the public gallery can be reused; please credit the original creator when sharing results.

Which models are supported?

Any image or video model that accepts text prompts can work; you simply copy the prompt into your favourite tool.
