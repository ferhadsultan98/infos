About

Aiprompt is a premium gallery for curated AI prompts (image & video) shared by the community.

Mission

Make prompt discovery fast, clean, and inspiring—without sacrificing design quality.

How it works

Browse, open details, copy prompts, and bookmark your favorites. Upload your own work after login.

Contact

For partnerships, feedback, and feature requests.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Why do I need to login?
Bookmarks, upload, and AI Generate are account-based features.
Can I upload videos?
Yes—MP4/MOV are supported via Cloudinary upload widget.
Why does the video stop when I navigate?
To prevent background playback and save battery/data.

Privacy & Terms

Short & clear summary for users.

Privacy

Aiprompt stores authentication info (Firebase) and user-generated content (prompts, bookmarks). Media uploads are handled via Cloudinary.

Terms

Do not upload copyrighted or illegal content. You are responsible for the prompts/media you publish.
